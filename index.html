// Process

How It Works

01
Browse Kits

Check out available project kits. Watch demo videos to see what you'll build.

02
Fill the Form

Submit a borrow request below with the kit name and your contact info.

03
Pick Up Kit

Come to our home to pick up the kit. Sign a simple pledge form confirming pickup and return dates.

04
Build & Return

Build your project, have fun, and return the kit with all components. Happy making! 🎉

💚
We Trust You!

Borrowing from Innovation Library is completely free. Please take good care of all the components in the kit — handle them with care, keep all parts together, and return everything in good condition. We believe in the maker community and trust you to do the right thing! 🙌

📋 When you pick up a kit you will sign a simple Borrower's Pledge confirming the kit name, pickup date, return date, and that you agree to return all components in good condition. No fees, no deposit — just trust!

// Contact

About & Contact

Amartya Haldar
Founder · Innovation Library

Hi! I'm Amartya, a middle schooler who loves building hardware projects. I have a bunch of cool maker kits — Arduino, Lego Spike, Sphero, micro:bit and more — and I thought, why not share them with other kids in the community?

That's how Innovation Library was born! My goal is to make hands-on tech accessible to every kid, for free. Borrow a kit, build something awesome, and inspire the next maker! 🚀
